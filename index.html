▪▪ Design & Engineering Philosophy

Designing websites that feel as clear as they are memorable.

I am a software engineering and computer science student bridging mathematical rigor with high-contrast frontend aesthetics. I believe exceptional web engineering does not rely on heavy dependencies — it stems from structural precision, semantic clarity, and deliberate layout geometry.

Currently undergoing dual undergraduate programs at Scaler School of Technology and BITS Pilani, I build web experiences with strict adherence to foundational standards: semantic HTML5 trees, modular CSS box modeling, flexible 1D alignments, and 2D spatial CSS Grid compositions.
